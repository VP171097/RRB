FAQ

Good to know

Short answers to the usual questions.

Is it free?

Yes. It is a free static website with no ads.

Do I need an account?

No. Your progress is saved in your browser. Sign in only if you want sync across devices or a study group.

Can I sign in with a phone number?

Yes: enter either an email or a phone number with a password. There are no OTP messages. A phone account can't reset a forgotten password, so remember it.

Is this official?

No. This is an independent study aid. Always confirm dates, vacancies and the syllabus on the official RRB notice.

Will it guarantee 90+?

Nothing can. It gives you a clear plan and daily structure; the marks come from you doing the work, so keep showing up.

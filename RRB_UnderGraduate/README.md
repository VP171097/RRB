# RRB NTPC Undergraduate

Same layout as `../RRB_Graduate`. Undergraduate shares the Maths, Reasoning and GA subject matter with Graduate, so `plan.js`, `notes.js`, `tricks.js` and `quiz.js` re-export the Graduate files. To give Undergraduate its own version of any of them, replace the re-export with real content.

Different from Graduate: `index.js` (pattern, typing test, eligibility note), `resources.js` (searches and books) and the syllabus level note.
